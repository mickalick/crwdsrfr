Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KM Makes LLC ("KM Makes," "we," "us," "our") handles information when you use CRWD SRFR (the "Site"), including CRWD SRFR accounts. It should be read alongside our Terms of Use.

You can still browse the calendar, the Board, acts and venues without an account or any personal information. If you create an account, we collect what we need to run it, and we've tried to keep that to a minimum. We don't sell your information and we don't use it for advertising.

1. Information We Collect

a) Your account: To create an account you give us:

  • Your email address, which we use to sign you in and to send the emails described in Section 3. It is never shown on your profile or to other members.
  • A username, which is public. It appears on your profile, posts and comments, and in other members' notifications.

We don't use passwords. You sign in with a code or link we email to you.

b) Your profile (all optional): display name, profile picture, header image (Max members), blurb, bio, area, anthem link, links, your About tab answers (such as Best/Worst/First, Top 5 Artists and custom prompts) and your Show History.

c) What you post and do on the Site:

  • Posts to the Board or your profile: titles, text, photos and videos, captions, and any acts, venue and show date you tag. Photos and videos can contain hidden details, such as where they were taken; consider removing location data before you upload.
  • Comments on posts and shows.
  • Hypes: the shows, acts, venues, posts and members ("SRFRs") you hype.
  • Messages and message requests you send and receive.
  • Blocks and reports you make.
  • Your settings, such as privacy and notification choices.

d) Max membership: Max is our optional paid membership. When Max payments are available, they will be handled by a third-party payment processor. We will not receive or store your full card or bank details, only what we need to manage your membership (for example, whether it's active and when it renews).

e) Donations via PayPal: If you choose to make a donation through the Site, you'll be directed to PayPal to complete the transaction. We do not collect or store your payment information, card details, or PayPal account information ourselves — that is handled entirely by PayPal under their own privacy policy. We may see limited, non-sensitive transaction confirmation (e.g., that a donation was made), but not your full financial details.

f) Cloudflare Web Analytics: We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall Site traffic (e.g., page views, general visit counts, referring sites, approximate geographic region, device/browser type). This tool is privacy-friendly by design:

  • It does not use cookies,
  • It does not track you across other websites,
  • It does not collect personal data or build a profile of individual visitors.

It gives us aggregate, anonymized insight into how the Site is used — not who you are.

g) Google Maps: The Site embeds Google Maps to display an interactive venue map. Google Maps is operated by Google LLC and may collect data about your interactions with the map (including IP address, browser type, and map usage) subject to Google's own privacy policy and cookie practices. Unlike Cloudflare Web Analytics, Google Maps does use cookies and may track interactions across Google's services. We do not control Google's data collection. You can review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

h) Technical logs: Like virtually any website, our hosting and infrastructure providers (see Section 6) automatically log basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps, for security and operational purposes. We look at these logs only to keep the Site and accounts secure and working, for example to stop spam or abuse.

2. Who Can See What

Your profile: Your username is always public. Who can see the rest of your profile depends on your Profile Visibility setting (Settings → Privacy):

  • Everyone: anyone, including people without an account,
  • Members: anyone signed in to an account,
  • Connections: members you have an accepted conversation with,
  • Nobody: only you.

Within your profile you can also hide your post list, each of your Hyped lists (shows, acts and venues), your area, when you joined, and your hype count.

Board posts and comments: Approved Board posts and your comments are public and show your username, whatever your profile settings are. Posts you make to your profile only follow your profile settings.

Board posts on our social media: We may feature photos and videos from Board posts on CRWD SRFR's social media accounts, such as Instagram, credited to your CRWD SRFR username, as described in our Terms of Use. Posts made only to your profile are never used this way. Once shared there, a post is also subject to that platform's own privacy policy. Contact us to have a featured post taken down.

Hypes: Hype counts on shows, acts, venues and posts include everyone's hypes but never list who gave them. Your Hyped shows, acts and venues appear on your profile only if you choose to share them. When you hype or comment on a post, its author, and others who commented on it, may see your username in their Lineup. Members who hype you may see the acts and venues you hype in their Lineup, if you share those lists. Who you hype as an SRFR is never shown to anyone, including them. They only see a total count.

Messages: Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation. They are stored securely on our provider's servers but are not end-to-end encrypted. We don't read your messages, except when a message is reported to us, when needed to investigate abuse or protect someone's safety, or when required by law.

Blocking: When you block someone, you and that person are hidden from each other across the Site, including profiles, posts, comments and messages.

Moderation: We review every post before it appears on the Site, and again after it's edited. We review the content and accounts involved in reports.

3. Emails We Send

Emails come from an @mail.crwdsrfr.com address and are delivered by our email provider, Resend (see Section 6).

a) Account emails: If you have an account, we'll email you when it's needed to use it or keep it secure: sign-in codes and links, confirming a change of email address, the code to delete your account, and important notices about your account or changes to our Terms or this Policy. These can't be turned off while you have an account.

b) Notification emails (optional): You choose these in Settings → Notifications:

  • New message requests: when someone sends you a message request (on by default),
  • New messages: when someone you're chatting with sends you a message (on by default),
  • Hype Report: a weekly email, on the day you choose, of new shows from the acts and venues you've hyped (Max members only; off by default).

You can turn each of these off at any time, and every notification email includes a link to your email settings. Notification emails say who is contacting you but don't include the message itself; read it on the Site.

These emails are about your own account and the things you've chosen to follow on the Site. Apart from them, we don't send promotional emails or newsletters unless you sign up for them. We don't track whether you open our emails or click the links in them. We never sell or share your email address.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Run the Site and your account, and show your content according to your settings,
  • Send the emails described in Section 3,
  • Keep the Site safe: review posts, handle reports, enforce our Terms of Use and prevent spam and abuse,
  • Understand aggregate Site usage and improve the experience,
  • Manage Max memberships and process voluntary donations,
  • Comply with the law.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes, and we don't build advertising profiles.

Sponsors and purchasers of featured placements on the Site may receive aggregate, anonymized performance data (e.g., total impressions, total clicks) related to their placements. Sponsors never receive personal data about individual visitors or members.

5. Cookies and Browser Storage

CRWD SRFR does not use cookies for tracking or advertising, and Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless. We do use your browser's storage for things the Site needs:

  • Keeping you signed in. If you choose "Keep me signed in," your sign-in is remembered for up to 30 days. Otherwise it ends when you close your browser.
  • Remembering your preferences, such as your calendar view and Board filters.

This information stays on your device and isn't used to track you. Signing out, or clearing your browser's site data, removes it.

The embedded Google Maps component may set cookies on your device as part of Google's standard Maps functionality. We do not control these cookies. If you prefer to avoid them, you can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect how the venue map functions. The Site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from public content networks (jsDelivr and the jQuery CDN), which receive your IP address and browser details when your browser requests them.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or retargeting tools of our own.

6. Service Providers

We use a small number of providers to run the Site. They handle information on our behalf, or under their own privacy policies where noted, and may process it in the United States or elsewhere:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and file storage. Your account, profile, posts, uploads, comments, hypes and messages are stored here.
  • Resend: sends our emails. It receives your email address and the content of the emails we send you.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare: hosting, content delivery, security and Cloudflare Web Analytics.
  • Google: the venue map (Google Maps) and fonts (Google Fonts), under Google's own privacy policy.
  • PayPal, for donations, and a payment processor for Max memberships once available, under their own privacy policies.

We may also disclose information if required by law or legal process, to protect the safety or rights of our members, the public or KM Makes, or as part of a merger, sale or transfer of the Site. If the Site is transferred, this Policy will continue to apply to your information unless you're told otherwise.

7. Your Choices and Controls

  • Edit or remove your profile information, posts, comments and hypes at any time.
  • Choose who sees what in Settings → Privacy and in Edit Profile, and who can send you message requests.
  • Choose which emails you get in Settings → Notifications.
  • Block or report any member, post, comment or message.
  • Download your data: Settings → Account → Download My Data gives you a copy of the information tied to your account.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete Account (we'll email you a code to confirm). Deleting your account permanently removes your account, profile, uploaded photos and videos, posts, comments, hypes, the messages you sent and the conversations you started. Reports you filed are kept for moderation records, without your name attached.

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, or to object to how it's used. Most of this can be done in Settings. For anything else, contact us (Section 12) and we'll respond within the time required by law.

8. How Long We Keep Information

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. Lineup updates about your posts and comments ("Ripples") are deleted automatically after 90 days. When you delete something, or your whole account, it is removed from the Site right away. Copies may remain in our providers' routine backups and logs for a limited time before they're overwritten. We may keep reports and moderation records, and information we're required to keep by law, for longer.

9. Children's Privacy

CRWD SRFR is not directed at children under 13, and you must be at least 13 years old to create an account. Members under 18 should have a parent or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we will delete it. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the Site, please contact us so we can address it. Many listed shows have age restrictions; always check a show's age policy with the venue.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Connections to the Site are encrypted, sign-in uses emailed one-time codes instead of passwords, and access to account data is limited by strict permissions so members can only see what this Policy describes. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. Keep your email account secure, since access to it allows access to your CRWD SRFR account.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. If we make significant changes, we'll let account holders know by email or on the Site before they take effect. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
